      How this works

      The fuel mix and carbon forecast for your area come from the Carbon Intensity API, which covers 14 regions of Great Britain. The stations on your map are the ones running right now, matched to the real high-voltage lines. Each fuel's share of your area is split between its stations, and bigger, closer ones count for more.

      Electricity mixes on the grid, so nobody can say which station reached a particular home. Treat the stations, routes and percentages as a reasonable estimate. The map uses the middle of your postcode district.

      Your address stays in your browser. Only the first half of your postcode is sent, to fetch the forecast.
