Privacy

This site doesn't use accounts, and it doesn't use cookies to follow you around.

Your address

Your full address and postcode stay in your browser. Only the first half of your postcode, such as SW9, leaves your device. It goes to the Carbon Intensity API to fetch your forecast, and it's added to an anonymous count of how many people look up each area. That count holds the district and the day, and nothing that links it to you.

Sharing

Share images and links only ever include the first half of your postcode.

Messages you send us

The contact form sends your message, and any reply details you add, to a private Telegram chat. We use them only to read and answer your message. Your IP address is scrambled and used briefly to stop spam, and isn't stored as an address.

On your device

Your answers about your tariff and car are saved in your browser so you don't have to enter them again. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Other services

The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when they download. If you use the coffee button, Ko-fi handles that under its own privacy policy.

Questions or removal

Use the contact form on the main page if you'd like us to delete a message you've sent.
